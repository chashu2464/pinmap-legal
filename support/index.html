常见问题

1. 为什么我看不到 iCloud 同步？

iCloud 同步功能可能依赖应用版本、系统设置以及相关功能是否已启用。请先确认设备已登录 Apple ID，且 iCloud 可用。

2. 为什么照片、视频或录音无法使用？

请检查系统是否已授予 PinMap 对照片、麦克风等权限的访问。如果之前拒绝过权限，可前往 iOS 的“设置”中重新开启。

3. 内购未解锁或恢复失败怎么办？

请确认当前 Apple ID 与购买账号一致，并尝试在应用内执行“恢复购买”。如问题仍存在，请通过邮件联系支持。

4. 如何反馈 bug 或功能建议？

请直接发送邮件到 casual2464@163.com，并尽量附上复现步骤与截图。

Frequently asked questions

1. Why is iCloud sync unavailable?

Please confirm that your device is signed in to Apple ID and that iCloud is available on the device. Some functionality may also depend on app version or feature availability.

2. Why can't I add photos, videos, or audio?

Please check whether PinMap has the required system permissions, such as Photos or Microphone access. If permission was denied earlier, you can re-enable it in iOS Settings.

3. What should I do if an in-app purchase doesn't unlock?

Make sure you're using the same Apple ID that completed the purchase, then try the Restore Purchases option in the app. If the issue persists, contact support by email.

4. How can I report a bug or request a feature?

Email casual2464@163.com with steps to reproduce, screenshots, and any other relevant details.
